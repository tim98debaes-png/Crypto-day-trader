# Phase 4 diagnostic contract

The diagnostic layer consumes the exact `BacktestResult` generated by the Step 1 execution engine. It does not alter signals, fills, fees, slippage, stops, targets, risk limits, or position sizing.

For each strategy it exports every audit event and the equity curve, then computes attribution by symbol, direction, exit reason and strategy tier. This keeps diagnosis separate from strategy optimization and makes the baseline reproducible before any candidate changes are proposed.
